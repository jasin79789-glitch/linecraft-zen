Privacy Policy

LineCraft Zen: One-Stroke Dot Connector

Amazon Appstore & Google AdMob Compliant
Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how LineCraft Zen: One-Stroke Dot Connector ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information when you play our mobile puzzle game on Android devices, Amazon Fire OS tablets, and other supported platforms.

We are firmly committed to user privacy. Our game is built around a tranquil, offline-capable Zen experience that does NOT require any personal account registration, logins, or collection of personal identities.

2. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

Local Gameplay Data: All your gameplay progression (unlocked levels, earned stars, best completion times, selected visual themes, and remaining hint counts) is saved strictly locally on your device via standard local storage. This data never leaves your device.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep LineCraft Zen free to play for everyone, the game incorporates third-party advertisements served by Google AdMob. These ads may include:

  • Adaptive Anchor Banners: Displayed with safety clearance at the bottom of the screen.
  • Interstitials: Infrequent interstitial ads between select level transitions.
  • Rewarded Videos: Optional, user-initiated videos to earn free hints.

Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers and technical telemetry to serve ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. This may include:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID) or Amazon Advertising ID (where applicable).
  • IP address and approximate geographic location (country or city level).
  • Device hardware model, operating system version, and mobile network information.

For more details on how Google uses information from apps that use its services, please visit:

Google Advertising Privacy & Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Your Advertising Choices & Opt-Out

You can opt out of personalized ads at any time through your device settings:

  • Amazon Fire Tablets: Go to Settings > Security & Privacy > Device Usage Data > Interest-Based Ads and toggle it off.
  • Android Devices: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or enable Opt out of Ads Personalization.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

LineCraft Zen is a general-audience geometric logic puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal data has inadvertently been collected, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

6. Security

Because the app does not transmit or store personal data on remote servers, your gameplay remains entirely private to your physical device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact us via our developer GitHub repository or developer contact page.
